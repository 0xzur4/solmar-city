Solmar is a 2D top-down open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked coastal city. Pick one of four characters, drive and steal cars, dodge the Solmar PD's wanted stars, roadblocks and police helicopter, run eleven story missions for the fixer Mara Voss, race checkpoints, wreck rival crews, take taxi fares and hunt ten hidden packages — all in plain HTML, canvas and WebAudio, no libraries and no network.

The city runs a full day cycle: bright day, golden hour, then night, when street lamps, headlights and neon signs take over. Repair your car at Mara's Garage, hide at the safehouse to lose the heat, and watch the wanted stars pulse as the chase heats up.

## Heroes

Everyone on the street is a chibi character with depth: a big head, a dark outline, gradient shading with a rim light, a soft ground shadow, eyes that follow your aim, a walk bob with squash and stretch, and a weapon that kicks back when it fires. Choose your crew member on the hero screen (arrows or mouse, Enter to confirm). Each has their own stats, starting weapon and a skill on **Q**:

| Hero | Role | Weapon | Skill |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dario Reyes | Street Boss | Street Pistol | **Adrenaline Rush**: fire 80% faster and run 15% quicker for 6s |
| Mika "Ghost" | Shadow Runner | Rattler SMG | **Shadow Dash**: dash through danger, untouchable for a moment; nitro burst in a car |
| Rocco Brandt | Heavy Bruiser | Boom Stick | **Ground Slam**: hurt and shove everything nearby |
| Dr. Luna Park | Street Medic | Ion Blaster | **Nano Burst**: heal 40 HP, mend your car, shrug off damage for 2s |

## Weapons

Four weapons, each with its own sound, tracer and ammo pool: Street Pistol, Rattler SMG (automatic, hold the button), Boom Stick (six-pellet shotgun) and Ion Blaster (plasma bolts that pierce). Pick up weapon crates around the city, switch with **1-4**, the mouse wheel or **X**, and fire from a car window for a drive-by.

## Controls

WASD move · Shift run · Click shoot (hold for automatic weapons) · Q hero skill · 1-4 / wheel / X weapons · F punch · E enter or exit a car · Space handbrake · G repair at the garage · P or Esc pause · M mute.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
